:::note[Runs in your browser]
This page uses the `nusaindex` package compiled into the page. What you type is checked on your device and is never sent anywhere: the site has no server, analytics, or third-party scripts. Still, prefer the **Fill fake data** button to typing real personal data.
:::

<Playground />

For a NIK, the result includes the region names from [`region.fromNik`](/reference/region/). Invalid values show the [error code](/guides/results-and-errors/) and, when the text contains full-width or zero-width characters, a hint to call [`normalize`](/guides/input-and-privacy/).
