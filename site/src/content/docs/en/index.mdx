## Safe by default

- **Offline.** No network calls, logging, or telemetry. Input never leaves your process.
- **Traceable.** Every dataset names its regulation and source document in [`data/manifest.json`](/en/project/data-sources/).
- **Unofficial.** Not affiliated with Dukcapil, DJP, Komdigi, Polri, Bank Indonesia, or any government agency. Valid means **well-formed**, not real or owned by anyone.
